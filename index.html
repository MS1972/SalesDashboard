Two tabs: a monthly client rollup with %Margin and Comments, and a multi-year quarterly view with Comments joined in.

What's automated: the monthly client rollup (current year) with a computed %Margin column, section subtotals, quarterly re-aggregation, and Comments joined by Sub-Group + Client. The Multi-Year tab keeps only Secured and Investments sections — Projection sections are left out. Money cells are whole-dollar with a $ sign, negatives in red with parentheses; section titles, subtotals, Actual (green), Margin (orange), the row's own Total/Margin/%Margin (gold), and Comments (blue) are colored for quick scanning.

What isn't: the multi-tier "TOTAL Secured / Projection / Prospecting" summary rows from a full reference report aren't rebuilt — you get a subtotal per section plus one grand total (Monthly Performance tab only). Prior-year figures are freshly computed from each year's own sheet (matched by Sub-Group + Client for the immediate prior year, best-effort name-matching for anything older) — they may not match a previously hand-adjusted figure if one exists, since older sheets don't always reconcile to their own SOW-level rows.
Built for repeat use — re-upload next month's workbook and re-run. Sheet names are matched loosely ("sales" + "customer" + year), so minor renames won't break it.
